# Mission Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, Microsoft Azure impressed me the most because of its strong integration with technologies commonly used by schools and businesses. Its connection with Windows Server, Microsoft 365, SQL Server, and Microsoft Entra ID can make migration and account management easier. I also appreciate that Azure supports both Windows and Linux virtual machines.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, object storage, databases, virtual networks, identity management, Kubernetes services, monitoring tools, and global infrastructure. They also use flexible pricing and allow organizations to increase or decrease resources based on demand. Although their service names are different, their basic purposes are often equivalent.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because every organization has different technologies, budgets, users, and goals. A company requiring a broad range of services may prefer AWS, while an organization already using Microsoft products may choose Azure. A research team focused on artificial intelligence, data analytics, or Kubernetes may find Google Cloud more appropriate. Therefore, the best provider is the one that solves the client's actual needs.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider requires analysis rather than simply selecting the most popular option. I learned how to compare equivalent services, study business scenarios, consider scalability and reliability, and explain why a particular platform is suitable. I also learned that one provider may be excellent for one client but less appropriate for another.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio became more organized and professional. It now contains separate research documents, comparison tables, client recommendations, Linux investigation results, official references, and screenshot evidence. The meaningful commit history also shows how I completed each checkpoint step by step. This activity improved my Markdown documentation, research, cloud-comparison, and decision-making skills.
